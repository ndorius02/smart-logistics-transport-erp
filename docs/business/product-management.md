# Product Management — Business Rules

## 1. Purpose

The **Product Management** module manages the product master data used throughout the Smart Logistics & Transport ERP.

A Product represents a business item that may later be:

- stored in one or more warehouses;
- purchased from suppliers;
- received into inventory;
- reserved for logistics operations;
- included in cargo;
- transported between locations;
- tracked through stock movements;
- reported in inventory and operational dashboards.

Product Management is responsible for the **identity and reference data of products**.

It does not manage physical stock quantities directly.

Stock quantities are handled separately by the **Inventory Management** module.

---

## 2. Product Definition

A product contains the following information:

- Technical identifier
- SKU
- Product name
- Description
- Product category
- Unit of measure
- Unit weight
- Active status
- Creation timestamp
- Last update timestamp

Example:

```text
SKU:             PRD-ELEC-001
Name:            Dell Latitude 5550
Category:        Electronics
UnitOfMeasure:   PIECE
Weight:          1.62 kg
Active:          true
```

---

## 3. Business Rules

### BR-PROD-001 — Product Identifier

Each product must have a unique technical identifier.

The identifier is generated by the application.

API clients must not provide or modify this identifier.

---

### BR-PROD-002 — SKU

Each product must have a SKU.

The SKU:

- is mandatory;
- must not be blank;
- must be unique;
- must identify the product consistently across the ERP.

Examples:

```text
PRD-ELEC-001
PRD-FOOD-001
PRD-AUTO-001
```

---

### BR-PROD-003 — SKU Normalization

Before persistence, the SKU must be:

- trimmed;
- converted to uppercase.

Example:

```text
" prd-elec-001 "
```

becomes:

```text
PRD-ELEC-001
```

---

### BR-PROD-004 — Product Name

Each product must have a name.

The name:

- is mandatory;
- must not be blank;
- represents the business-readable product designation.

Examples:

```text
Dell Latitude 5550
Industrial Safety Helmet
Frozen Salmon Box
Pallet Wrap Film
```

---

### BR-PROD-005 — Description

A product may have an optional description.

The description provides additional business information about the product.


---

### BR-PROD-006 — Product Category

Each product must belong to exactly one ProductCategory.

Relationship:

```text
ProductCategory
      1
      |
      |
      *
   Product
```

A ProductCategory may contain many products.

A Product belongs to exactly one ProductCategory.

---

### BR-PROD-007 — Product Category Must Exist

The category referenced when creating or updating a Product must exist in the database.

If the supplied category identifier does not exist, the operation must fail with a business error.

---

### BR-PROD-008 — Product Category Must Be Active

A new Product must not be assigned to an inactive ProductCategory.

The backend must verify that:

```text
ProductCategory.active = true
```

before accepting the category.

Existing products already assigned to a category remain valid if that category is later deactivated.

---

### BR-PROD-009 — Unit of Measure

Each Product must have a UnitOfMeasure.

Supported values for V1 are:

```text
PIECE
BOX
PALLET
KILOGRAM
LITER
METER
```

The unit of measure is implemented as a controlled Java enum.

This prevents inconsistent values such as:

```text
kg
KG
Kilo
Kilogram
kilograms
```

---

### BR-PROD-010 — Unit Weight

A product may have a unit weight.

When provided:

- weight must be greater than zero;
- weight is expressed in kilograms.

Example:

```text
1.62
```

means:

```text
1.62 kg per product unit
```

---

### BR-PROD-011 — Product Active Status

A newly created Product must be active by default.

```text
active = true
```

The initial active status is controlled by the backend.

The API client must not choose the initial status during creation.

---

### BR-PROD-012 — Product Deactivation

Products must not be physically deleted through normal business operations.

A Product may instead be deactivated.

Deactivation preserves:

- inventory history;
- warehouse stock references;
- procurement history;
- stock movement history;
- shipment history;
- audit information.

---

### BR-PROD-013 — Product Reactivation

An inactive Product may be reactivated by an authorized user.

Reactivation must preserve:

- Product ID;
- SKU;
- existing inventory references;
- historical transactions.

Reactivation does not create a new Product.

---

### BR-PROD-014 — Inactive Product Usage

An inactive Product remains readable for historical purposes.

However, inactive products must not be used for new operational transactions where active products are required.

Examples of future restricted operations include:

- new purchase orders;
- new inventory receipts;
- new stock reservations;
- new cargo allocations.

---

### BR-PROD-015 — Product Update

Authorized users may update:

- SKU;
- name;
- description;
- ProductCategory;
- UnitOfMeasure;
- unit weight;
- active status.

The technical identifier must never change.

---

### BR-PROD-016 — Updating SKU Must Preserve Uniqueness

During update:

- the Product may retain its existing SKU;
- another Product's SKU cannot be reused.

Uniqueness checks must exclude the Product currently being updated.

---

### BR-PROD-017 — Product Master Data and Inventory Are Separate

Product must not directly contain stock quantities.

The Product entity must not contain fields such as:

```text
quantity
reservedQuantity
availableQuantity
warehouseId
minimumStockLevel
```

These values belong to Inventory Management.

---

### BR-PROD-018 — Product May Exist in Multiple Warehouses

The same Product may be stored in multiple warehouses.

Example:

```text
Product: PRD-ELEC-001

Warehouse Brussels
Quantity: 50

Warehouse Antwerp
Quantity: 120

Warehouse Liege
Quantity: 30
```

Product master data remains the same regardless of warehouse.

Inventory manages the warehouse-specific quantities.

---

### BR-PROD-019 — Audit Information

The application must maintain:

```text
createdAt
updatedAt
```

`createdAt` is assigned when the Product is created.

`updatedAt` is updated whenever the Product is modified.

These fields are managed by the existing `Auditable` base entity.

API clients must not provide or modify them.

---

## 4. Product Search Rules

Users must be able to search products by:

- SKU;
- product name;
- ProductCategory.

Search operations must:

- support pagination;
- be case-insensitive where applicable;
- support partial matching for SKU and name.

---

### Search by SKU

Example:

```http
GET /api/products/search/sku?sku=ELEC
```

Possible results:

```text
PRD-ELEC-001
PRD-ELEC-002
PRD-ELEC-010
```

---

### Search by Product Name

Example:

```http
GET /api/products/search/name?name=dell
```

Possible result:

```text
Dell Latitude 5550
Dell Precision Workstation
```

---

### Filter by Product Category

Products must be retrievable by ProductCategory.

Example:

```http
GET /api/products/category/{categoryId}
```

This operation returns products belonging to the specified category.

---

## 5. Pagination

Product list and search endpoints must support pagination.

Supported parameters:

```text
page
size
sort
```

Example:

```http
GET /api/products?page=0&size=10&sort=name,asc
```

---

## 6. Product Lifecycle

A Product follows this lifecycle:

```text
       CREATE
         |
         v
       ACTIVE
         |
         | deactivate
         v
      INACTIVE
         |
         | activate
         v
       ACTIVE
```

There is no physical delete operation in V1.

## 10. UnitOfMeasure

For Product Management V1, UnitOfMeasure is represented as a Java enum.

Expected enum:

```java
public enum UnitOfMeasure {
    PIECE,
    BOX,
    PALLET,
    KILOGRAM,
    LITER,
    METER
}
```

Unlike ProductCategory, UnitOfMeasure is treated as a stable controlled technical value rather than independently managed business master data.

---

## 11. ProductCategory Relationship

ProductCategory is maintained independently from Product.

Conceptual relationship:

```text
ProductCategory
      |
      | 1
      |
      | *
   Product
```

Database representation:

```text
product_categories
        |
        | id
        |
        v
products.category_id
```

The Product table will therefore contain:

```text
category_id UUID NOT NULL
```

with a foreign key to:

```text
product_categories(id)
```

---

## 12. Relationship with Inventory Management

Product Management does not manage physical stock.

Inventory Management will later introduce:

```text
Product
      1
      |
      |
      *
 Inventory
      *
      |
      |
      1
 Warehouse
```

Each Inventory record will represent the stock state of one Product in one Warehouse.

Example:

```text
Product
PRD-ELEC-001
Dell Latitude 5550

Warehouse Brussels
quantity = 50

Warehouse Antwerp
quantity = 120
```

---

## 13. Product Master Data vs Inventory Data

The responsibilities are intentionally separated.

### Product Management

Answers:

```text
What is this product?
```

Examples:

```text
SKU
Name
Description
Category
Unit of Measure
Weight
Status
```

### Inventory Management

Answers:

```text
Where is this product stored and how much is available?
```

Examples:

```text
Warehouse
Physical quantity
Reserved quantity
Available quantity
Minimum stock level
```

This separation prevents product reference data from becoming coupled to warehouse-specific stock information.

## 16. Summary

Product is a **master-data entity** representing the identity and business characteristics of items handled by the ERP.

Its responsibilities are:

- maintaining unique SKUs;
- maintaining product descriptions;
- associating products with ProductCategory;
- maintaining controlled units of measure;
- supporting activation and deactivation;
- supporting searchable and pageable product records;
- providing stable product references for Inventory, Procurement, Cargo, Shipment and Reporting modules.

Product does not manage warehouse stock directly.

Warehouse-specific stock is the responsibility of the future Inventory Management module.
