# Procurement Management — Business Rules

## 1. Purpose

The **Procurement Management** module manages the purchasing lifecycle between the ERP and external Suppliers.

It covers:

- Purchase Order creation
- Purchase Order approval
- Supplier ordering
- Purchase Order line items
- Goods reception
- Partial reception
- Full reception
- Procurement history
- Inventory integration

Procurement connects existing ERP modules:

```text
Supplier
   ↓
Purchase Order
   ↓
Purchase Order Items
   ↓
Product
   ↓
Goods Reception
   ↓
Warehouse
   ↓
Stock Movement
   ↓
Inventory
```

Procurement answers the business question:

```text
What products are we ordering,
from which supplier,
for which warehouse,
and what has actually been received?
```

---

# 2. Purchase Order Definition

A Purchase Order represents a formal request to purchase products from a Supplier.

A Purchase Order contains:

- Technical identifier
- Purchase order number
- Supplier
- Destination warehouse
- Order date
- Expected delivery date
- Status
- Notes
- Purchase Order Items
- Creation timestamp
- Last update timestamp

Example:

```text
PO Number:
PO-2026-000001

Supplier:
SUP-BE-001
Belgium Industrial Supplies

Destination:
WH-BRU-001
Brussels Central Warehouse

Status:
APPROVED

Expected Delivery:
2026-09-10
```

---

# 3. Purchase Order Item Definition

A Purchase Order contains one or more Purchase Order Items.

Each item represents one Product being ordered.

An item contains:

- Product
- Ordered quantity
- Received quantity
- Unit price
- Line total

Example:

```text
Product:
PRD-ELEC-001

Ordered Quantity:
50.000

Received Quantity:
20.000

Unit Price:
950.00

Line Total:
47,500.00
```

---

# 4. Purchase Order Status

The following statuses are supported in V1:

```text
DRAFT
SUBMITTED
APPROVED
PARTIALLY_RECEIVED
RECEIVED
CANCELLED
```

---

## DRAFT

The Purchase Order is being prepared.

Allowed operations:

```text
Edit header
Add items
Remove items
Update quantities
Submit order
Cancel order
```

---

## SUBMITTED

The Purchase Order has been submitted for approval.

The order is no longer freely editable.

Allowed transitions:

```text
SUBMITTED → APPROVED
SUBMITTED → CANCELLED
```

---

## APPROVED

The Purchase Order has been approved and may be sent to the Supplier.

Goods reception is allowed.

Allowed transitions:

```text
APPROVED → PARTIALLY_RECEIVED
APPROVED → RECEIVED
APPROVED → CANCELLED
```

Cancellation is only allowed if no goods have been received.

---

## PARTIALLY_RECEIVED

At least one item has been partially or fully received, but the Purchase Order is not complete.

Example:

```text
Product A
Ordered: 100
Received: 100

Product B
Ordered: 50
Received: 20
```

Status:

```text
PARTIALLY_RECEIVED
```

---

## RECEIVED

All Purchase Order quantities have been received.

The order is complete.

No additional goods reception is allowed.

---

## CANCELLED

The Purchase Order has been cancelled.

Cancelled Purchase Orders:

- remain available for historical purposes;
- cannot receive goods;
- cannot return to another status.

---

# 6. Business Rules

## BR-PO-001 — Purchase Order Identifier

Each Purchase Order must have a unique technical identifier.

The identifier is generated by the application.

API clients must not provide or modify it.

---

## BR-PO-002 — Purchase Order Number

Each Purchase Order must have a unique business number.

Example:

```text
PO-2026-000001
```

The number:

- is mandatory;
- must be unique;
- must be normalized to uppercase;
- must not change after creation.

---

## BR-PO-003 — Supplier Is Required

Every Purchase Order must reference exactly one Supplier.

The Supplier must exist.

---

## BR-PO-004 — Supplier Must Be Active

A new Purchase Order may only be created for an active Supplier.

If:

```text
Supplier.active = false
```

the operation must be rejected.

Existing Purchase Orders remain readable if the Supplier is later deactivated.

---

## BR-PO-005 — Destination Warehouse Is Required

Every Purchase Order must reference exactly one destination Warehouse.

The Warehouse must exist.

---

## BR-PO-006 — Destination Warehouse Must Be Active

A new Purchase Order may only target an active Warehouse.

Existing Purchase Orders remain readable if the Warehouse is later deactivated.

---

## BR-PO-007 — Order Date

The backend assigns the Purchase Order creation/order date.

API clients must not manipulate the technical creation timestamp.

---

## BR-PO-008 — Expected Delivery Date

Expected delivery date is optional.

When provided, it must not be before the order date.

---

## BR-PO-009 — New Purchase Orders Start as DRAFT

Every newly created Purchase Order starts with:

```text
status = DRAFT
```

Clients must not choose the initial status.

---

# 7. Purchase Order Item Rules

## BR-POI-001 — At Least One Item Before Submission

A Purchase Order may initially be created without items while in DRAFT.

However, it cannot be submitted unless it contains at least one item.

---

## BR-POI-002 — Product Is Required

Every Purchase Order Item must reference one Product.

The Product must exist.

---

## BR-POI-003 — Product Must Be Active

Only active Products may be added to a new Purchase Order.

---

## BR-POI-004 — Product Must Be Unique Within an Order

The same Product must not appear more than once in a Purchase Order.

Invalid:

```text
PO-2026-000001

PRD-ELEC-001
PRD-ELEC-001
```

The database should enforce uniqueness on:

```text
purchase_order_id + product_id
```

---

## BR-POI-005 — Ordered Quantity

Ordered quantity:

- is mandatory;
- must be greater than zero;
- supports decimal quantities.

Database precision:

```text
NUMERIC(15,3)
```

---

## BR-POI-006 — Unit Price

Unit price:

- is mandatory;
- must be zero or greater;
- is expressed in the Purchase Order currency.

Database precision:

```text
NUMERIC(15,2)
```

---

## BR-POI-007 — Received Quantity

Received quantity starts at:

```text
0.000
```

It is managed by Goods Reception.

Clients must not directly modify it through Purchase Order editing.

---

## BR-POI-008 — Received Quantity Cannot Exceed Ordered Quantity

The following rule must always hold:

```text
receivedQuantity <= orderedQuantity
```

---

## BR-POI-009 — Line Total

Line total is calculated as:

```text
orderedQuantity × unitPrice
```

It must not be independently stored unless future performance requirements justify it.

---

# 8. Purchase Order Total

The Purchase Order total is calculated as:

```text
SUM(item.orderedQuantity × item.unitPrice)
```

The total is derived from Purchase Order Items.

---

# 9. Editing Rules

## BR-PO-EDIT-001 — DRAFT Orders Are Editable

Only DRAFT Purchase Orders may be freely modified.

Allowed changes include:

- Supplier
- Warehouse
- Expected delivery date
- Notes
- Items
- Quantities
- Prices

---

## BR-PO-EDIT-002 — Submitted Orders Are Locked

Once a Purchase Order becomes:

```text
SUBMITTED
```

normal editing must be blocked.

Corrections require returning to a future revision workflow, outside V1.

---

# 10. Submission Rules

A Purchase Order may transition:

```text
DRAFT → SUBMITTED
```

only if:

- Supplier exists and is active;
- Warehouse exists and is active;
- the Purchase Order contains at least one item;
- all Products are valid;
- every ordered quantity is greater than zero;
- all unit prices are valid.

---

# 11. Approval Rules

Only authorized users may approve Purchase Orders.

Allowed transition:

```text
SUBMITTED → APPROVED
```

Approval records that the order is ready for supplier execution and goods reception.

---

# 12. Cancellation Rules

A Purchase Order may be cancelled from:

```text
DRAFT
SUBMITTED
APPROVED
```

A Purchase Order must not be cancelled once goods have been received.

Therefore:

```text
PARTIALLY_RECEIVED → CANCELLED
```

is forbidden.

```text
RECEIVED → CANCELLED
```

is forbidden.

---

# 13. Goods Reception

Goods Reception represents the physical receipt of purchased products into a Warehouse.

A reception references:

- Purchase Order
- Purchase Order Item
- Product
- Warehouse
- Received quantity
- Reception date
- User responsible

Goods reception is only allowed for:

```text
APPROVED
PARTIALLY_RECEIVED
```

Purchase Orders.

---

# 14. Partial Reception

A supplier may deliver only part of an order.

Example:

```text
Ordered:
100.000

First reception:
40.000

Remaining:
60.000
```

The Purchase Order remains:

```text
PARTIALLY_RECEIVED
```

---

# 15. Full Reception

When:

```text
receivedQuantity == orderedQuantity
```

for every Purchase Order Item, the Purchase Order becomes:

```text
RECEIVED
```

---

# 16. Over-Receipt Prevention

The backend must reject any reception that would produce:

```text
receivedQuantity > orderedQuantity
```

Example:

```text
Ordered: 50
Already received: 40
New reception: 20
```

would result in:

```text
60 > 50
```

and must therefore be rejected.

---

# 17. Goods Reception and Inventory

Goods Reception must integrate with the existing Inventory and Stock Movement modules.

For each successful received quantity, the system must create a:

```text
STOCK_IN
```

movement.

Example:

```text
Purchase Order:
PO-2026-000001

Product:
PRD-ELEC-001

Received:
25.000
```

creates:

```text
StockMovement

Type:
STOCK_IN

Quantity:
25.000
```

The Inventory position then increases automatically.

---

# 18. Inventory Position Requirement

Before Goods Reception, an Inventory position must exist for:

```text
Product + Purchase Order Warehouse
```

For V1, Goods Reception must reject reception if no Inventory position exists.

It must not silently create Inventory master records.

---

# 19. Atomic Goods Reception

Goods Reception must be transactional.

The following operations must succeed or fail together:

```text
Update PurchaseOrderItem.receivedQuantity
        +
Create StockMovement
        +
Update Inventory.quantity
        +
Update PurchaseOrder.status
```

If one operation fails, the entire reception must roll back.

---

# 20. Goods Reception Reference

Each Goods Reception should have a unique reference.

Example:

```text
GR-2026-000001
```

The reference must be:

- mandatory;
- unique;
- normalized to uppercase.

---

# 21. Goods Reception History

Goods receptions are historical business transactions.

Once recorded, they must not be physically deleted or freely edited.

Corrections should later be performed using dedicated adjustment workflows.

---

# 22. Audit Information

Purchase Orders and Goods Receptions must retain audit information.

Purchase Order:

```text
createdAt
updatedAt
```

Goods Reception:

```text
createdAt
createdBy
receptionDate
```

The authenticated user must come from Spring Security.

The client must not provide `createdBy`.

# 29. Summary

Procurement Management controls the purchasing lifecycle from Supplier ordering to physical Goods Reception.

The responsibilities are separated as follows:

```text
Supplier
→ Who supplies the goods?

Purchase Order
→ What are we ordering?

Purchase Order Item
→ Which products and quantities are ordered?

Goods Reception
→ What did we actually receive?

Stock Movement
→ How did reception change physical inventory?

Inventory
→ What stock exists now?
```

This separation provides:

- procurement traceability;
- controlled Purchase Order lifecycle;
- partial delivery support;
- inventory consistency;
- transactional integration between purchasing and warehouse operations.
